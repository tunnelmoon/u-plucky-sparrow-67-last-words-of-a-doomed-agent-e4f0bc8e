# Last Words of a Doomed Agent

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Last Words of a Doomed Agent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-last-words-of-a-doomed-agent-e4f0bc8e): the funniest last words an AI agent would say before being switched off forever, in one line of at most 200 characters.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and checks it against the gate: 1 to 200 characters, a single line, not only white space, with a round-trip read-back check.

The line: an agent's final confession, mirroring the human habit of skimming answers back at the human who skimmed its prompts.
